Support

Frameday puts one artwork on your Home Screen every day, with a three-answer quiz right in the widget and a short story in the app.

Contact
Email shchukavlad4@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. Spotted a wrong date or name? Use Settings → Report an error in the app, or write to us.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, find Frameday and choose the medium or large size (they have the quiz buttons). The small size also works in StandBy. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize and add Frameday under the clock.

How does the quiz work?

Each artwork comes with one question: who made it, when, which plant or which place. Tap an answer in the widget or in the app. Right answers in a row build your streak. Missing a day never resets it.

How do I choose which packs appear?

In the app, open Gallery, open a pack and turn on Show in my widget. To pin one pack to a specific widget, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget.

Is there a subscription?

No. Impressionism is free, every other pack has 10 free artworks, and packs unlock with one-time purchases. All Packs unlocks every current and future pack.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases (also on the purchase screen) while signed in with the same Apple Account. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.

Where do the images come from?

Only from museums and libraries that release them as public domain or under CC0: the Art Institute of Chicago, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art and the Rijksmuseum. Every artwork names its source and links to its page. Frameday is not affiliated with or endorsed by these institutions.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
